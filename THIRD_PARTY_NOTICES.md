## AE2 Lightning Tech

The Craftline Provisioner wireless target binding workflow includes adapted ideas and source-code
structures from AE2 Lightning Tech's overloaded wireless connector and pattern-provider connection
implementation. The adaptation is limited to the interaction state machine, one-face-per-target
connection model, and world-space binding visualization; no AE2 Lightning Tech textures are included.
