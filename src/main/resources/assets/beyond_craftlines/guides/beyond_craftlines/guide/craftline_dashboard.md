# Craftline Dashboard

Place the dashboard against a solid face and connect it to a Beyond Dimensions network. Right-click it to configure its virtual target. JEI ingredients can be dragged into the highlighted ghost slot; items, fluids, and registered chemicals are accepted without consuming anything.

Choose whether the maintained amount lives in the BD network or in the attached container face. Container mode sends existing BD network stock to the attached face first and orders only the remainder; a blocked face pauses without submitting another order or trying another side. Redstone modes are Ignore, Low, High, and Rising Edge.

The recipe-tree button opens the normal Craftlines tree. Its amount field becomes the maintained amount, the destination button switches between network and container monitoring, the blocking-mode button retains its normal meaning, and Save writes the fixed choices back to the dashboard.

Automatic orders are shown together with manual orders in the network status screen. They do not consume a player's order limit; by default, one network may have ten active automatic orders.
